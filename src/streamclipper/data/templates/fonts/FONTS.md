All fonts are free to bundle and redistribute. Each licence text is in `licenses/` (the four older fonts share `OFL.txt`).
Sources are the official Google Fonts repository, https://github.com/google/fonts (downloaded 16 Nov 2026).

The emoji font is used only in MP4 captions (ASS). When it is missing, emoji are left out of the MP4 captions,
never drawn as empty boxes. CapCut drafts use CapCut's own emoji rendering.
